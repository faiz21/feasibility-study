Section 01

Executive Summary

Plant Context & Assessment Scope

PT Indonesia Asahan Aluminium (INALUM) operates the Green Plant as the front-end carbon preparation and green anode forming stage. This assessment evaluates automation maturity across L1–L3 layers to determine feasibility for phased digitalisation.

Structural Realities Observed

  • 1

    Multi-brand PLC estate (Rockwell, Siemens, Omron) with uneven integration.

  • 2

    Significant manual RPM settings in core solids handling.

  • 3

    Instrument accuracy issues, including 12% belt scale variance.

Overall maturity indicates L1 is partially established, L2 is fragmented, and L3 readiness is limited.

Section 06

Plant-wide Findings

01

Operator Dependency

Bulk solids handling relies on manual RPM settings and manual dampers.

02

Degrading Data Trust

Dust accumulation degrades silo level sensors, forcing parallel manual checks.

03

Integrity Gaps

Critical conveyors lack belt-tear or vibration sensing, increasing reactive failure risk.

Strategic Implication

"L1 completion and sensor trust restoration are non-negotiable prerequisites before scaling L2/L3 dashboards".

Implementation Roadmap

Phase 0 (3–4M)

Verification

Update asset maps, validate critical sensors, and close documentation gaps.

Phase 1 (6–9M)

L1 Hardening

Restore protective sensing and integrate relay islands into PLC logic.

Phase 2 (6–9M)

L2 Visibility

Consolidate SCADA views and implement plant-wide alarm logging.

Phase 3 (9–12M)

L3 Intelligence

Deploy structured stop-cause logging and automated performance reporting.

Critical Technical Risks

Multi-platform PLC governance increases lifecycle and integration complexity.

Absence of event history limits the efficacy of root cause analysis programs.
