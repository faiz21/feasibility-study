INA	PT Indonesia Asahan Alumunium	Company Snapshot

PT Indonesia Asahan Aluminium (INALUM) is Indonesia’s state-owned integrated aluminum producer operating in the Asahan region of North Sumatra. The company leverages hydropower from the Lake Toba system and proximity to the Malacca Strait for energy-intensive smelting and export logistics. INALUM operates multiple major facilities covering the full aluminum value chain from carbon production to casting, with current capacity around 260 kilotons per year and an announced expansion target of approximately 1,000 kilotons per year.

Products & Customers

INALUM’s primary products include aluminum ingots with purity ranges of approximately 99.7–99.92%, aluminum billets (5–8 inch), and foundry alloys such as A356.2. Customers typically include downstream aluminum processors, automotive and industrial manufacturers, and export buyers across Asia. Specific key customer names are to validate with client.

Value Chain Position

INALUM operates as a Tier 2 player in the aluminum value chain, supplying primary aluminum products to downstream rolling, extrusion, and component manufacturers rather than end consumers.

Production spans carbon baking, rodding, electrolytic reduction (potlines), casting, and finishing. End-to-End Process Narrative

Inbound alumina and process additives are received, stored, and fed to the reduction area. In parallel, petroleum coke and pitch are processed into green anode blocks, baked to form conductive prebake anodes, then rodded (steel rod + cast-iron connection) to create anode assemblies for potline consumption. In the reduction plant, alumina is dissolved in a molten cryolite-based electrolyte and reduced to molten aluminum metal using high-current DC power. Molten aluminum is periodically tapped, transported to holding furnaces, treated to control cleanliness (dross removal, filtration, degassing), and chemistry-adjusted where required to produce alloys. The metal is then cast into ingots or billets, cooled, cut/finished, packaged, and staged for shipment. Off-gases and particulates from smelting and carbon operations are treated in gas cleaning systems, and recyclable streams (e.g., spent anode butts) are routed back into the process where applicable (To validate — recycle loop boundaries and ratios).

Major Manufacturing Stages (numbered 1…N; as detailed as possible)

1. Inbound receiving & bulk storage (alumina, carbon raws, additives, consumables)
Purpose: ensure continuous feed availability and controlled quality/segregation.
Unit operations: unloading, sampling, silo/yard storage, reclaiming, conveying, lot/grade segregation.
Transformation: none (material handling/conditioning).
Key outputs: available feed lots for carbon plant and reduction; inspection status (To validate — QC release mechanism).
2. Coke preparation & proportioning (carbon raw preparation)
Purpose: create consistent coke fractions for predictable green anode properties.
Unit operations: crushing, screening/classification, dust handling, fraction blending, return-carbon handling (butts/rejects).
Transformation: physical size reduction/classification.
Key outputs: proportioned coke recipe streams; fines/dust stream for controlled reuse/disposal.
3. Paste mixing / kneading (green mix preparation)
Purpose: bind coke recipe with pitch to form homogeneous anode paste.
Unit operations: pitch heating, controlled addition, intensive mixing/kneading, temperature control, recycle dosing.
Transformation: physical mixing + thermal softening of binder; wetting/dispersion.
Key outputs: homogeneous green mix at target temperature/viscosity.
4. Green anode forming & handling (Green Plant)
Purpose: form green blocks with controlled density/geometry for baking.
Unit operations: forming/compaction (press/vibrocompaction), demolding, cooling, trimming, green scrap segregation, transfer to baking.
Transformation: physical densification/shape formation.
Key outputs: green blocks; green scrap/returns stream.
5. Anode baking (Baking Plant)
Purpose: carbonize pitch binder and develop anode strength/conductivity for stable potline operation.
Unit operations: furnace loading, packing coke management, controlled heat-up/soak/cool-down, fume collection interface, unloading/inspection.
Transformation: thermal pyrolysis/carbonization; densification and property development.
Key outputs: baked anodes meeting property specs; baked rejects/cracked blocks for recycle.
6. Rodding & anode assembly (Rodding Plant)
Purpose: create rodded anode assemblies with low electrical resistance and mechanical integrity.
Unit operations: anode stub-hole prep, rod positioning, cast iron pouring, cooling, cleaning, inspection, staging for potroom; butt/stub recovery interface.
Transformation: mechanical assembly + molten metal casting (cast iron connection).
Key outputs: rodded anode assemblies; rodding rejects; recovered butts/stubs streams.
7. Electrolytic reduction / potline operation (Reduction Plant)
Purpose: reduce alumina to molten aluminum continuously with controlled bath chemistry and thermal balance.
Unit operations: alumina feeding, bath chemistry control (AlF₃/cryolite balance), anode effect management, anode changes, tapping metal, metal/bath level control, crust management.
Transformation: electrochemical reduction producing molten Al; consumption of carbon anodes.
Key outputs: molten aluminum (tapped); spent anode butts; potline off-gas to gas cleaning.
8. Molten metal tapping, transport, and holding
Purpose: transfer molten safely and maintain metallurgical condition before casting.
Unit operations: tapping into ladles/crucibles, temperature measurement, skimming/dross handling, transfer to holding furnaces, hold/temperature equalization.
Transformation: none (thermal conditioning; oxidation/dross formation as side effect).
Key outputs: conditioned molten metal for treatment/casting; dross stream.
9. Melt treatment & metallurgy control (Cast house)
Purpose: meet cleanliness and chemistry requirements for ingot/billet/alloy products.
Unit operations: alloying additions, fluxing/degassing (gas injection/rotary), filtration, sampling for chemistry, inclusion control, furnace/holding management.
Transformation: chemical composition adjustment; gas/inclusion removal.
Key outputs: treated melt at target chemistry/cleanliness; spent flux; treatment dross.
10. Casting (ingot, billet, foundry alloy) + cooling + finishing
Purpose: solidify melt into saleable forms with controlled dimensions and properties.
Unit operations: mold or DC casting, cooling, cut-to-length, stacking, marking/heat stamping, bundling/packaging.
Transformation: phase change (liquid → solid) with controlled solidification conditions.
Key outputs: finished ingots/billets/alloy products; runners/sprues/returns; casting scrap.
11. Gas cleaning, dust handling, and recycle loops (Gas Cleaning Plant + interfaces)
Purpose: capture particulates/fluorides and manage emissions and solids.
Unit operations: ducting, filtration/scrubbing, fan/pressure control, dust collection, solids handling, recycle routing where applicable.
Transformation: separation (gas/solid) and capture.
Key outputs: treated exhaust; collected dust/solids (some recyclable To validate); compliant discharge streams.
12. Quality assurance & release (Smelter QA + casting QA)
Purpose: verify compliance to product and process specifications and authorize release/hold.
Unit operations: sampling plans, lab analysis (chemistry), defect/nonconformance handling, certification data preparation.
Transformation: none (measurement/decision support).
Key outputs: test results, release status, certificates/COA support (To validate — certification standards).

9. Melt treatment: alloy addition mass accuracy; melt temperature; degassing time/flow; filtration ΔP/condition; hydrogen content proxy (To validate); inclusion count proxy (To validate).
10. Casting + cooling + finishing: casting speed; mold/DC cooling water flow; metal temperature at cast; solidification control (thermal gradient); defect rate (porosity/cracks); cut length tolerance; bundling tension/pack integrity.
11. Gas cleaning & dust: fan speed/pressure; filter differential pressure; capture efficiency proxy; dust handling rate; recycle routing control (To validate).
12. Quality assurance: sampling frequency; lab turnaround time; assay precision/accuracy; hold/release criteria thresholds (To validate); traceability link integrity (heat/lot).

By Stage — Quality Drivers

1. Inbound receiving & storage: consistent feed quality and segregation; uninterrupted availability; low contamination.
2. Coke preparation & proportioning: stable recipe control; minimized variability to protect downstream anode properties.
3. Paste mixing / kneading: homogeneous binder distribution; stable paste rheology for forming consistency.
4. Green anode forming: target green density and low defect rate to prevent bake cracks and potline instability.
5. Anode baking: baked anode resistivity/density within spec; low crack rate; stable bake yield.
6. Rodding & assembly: low electrical contact resistance; robust mechanical connection; minimal assembly defects.
7. Potline reduction: stable operation (low anode effects); high current efficiency; controlled impurity pickup; predictable tapping quality.
8. Tapping/transport/holding: minimal temperature loss and oxidation; controlled dross; safe and repeatable transfer.
9. Melt treatment: chemistry within grade; low dissolved gas; low inclusions; consistent cleanliness before casting.
10. Casting + finishing: dimensional compliance; surface quality; low internal defects; reliable marking/traceability and pack integrity.
11. Gas cleaning: compliant emissions; reliable capture; safe solids handling and controlled recycle/disposal.
12. Quality assurance: accurate chemistry certification; fast containment of nonconformance; traceable release decisions.

Plantwide — Energy & Utility Drivers

* Power availability/stability (MW, voltage/current quality) as the primary throughput limiter for potlines and rectifiers.
* Cooling water reliability to protect casting stability, equipment life, and furnace operations.

* Fuel/thermal energy availability for baking/furnace needs (To validate — dependency by plant).
* Utility water quality + WWTP performance to maintain compliance and protect equipment from scaling/corrosion.

Plantwide — Reliability, Safety, and Compliance Drivers

* Reliability: rectifier/transformer criticality, potline equipment uptime, furnace availability, crane/handling uptime, spare parts for high-critical assets, disciplined shutdown/startup procedures.
* Safety: molten metal handling controls, high-temperature exposure controls, lifting/rigging discipline, electrical HV/MV safety, gas/dust exposure controls, permit-to-work for maintenance in high-risk zones.
* Compliance: emissions control (fluoride/particulate), wastewater discharge compliance, waste/solid handling governance, traceable quality records and product certification (To validate — ISO/certifications in scope).
