## 3. Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### 3.1 PLC Environment [L-D1]

Score: 2. Label: Basic. Evidence basis: Obsolete S7-300 controllers and retrofit flags across core process groups.

### 3.2 Supervisory Layer [L-D2]

Score: 2. Label: Basic. Evidence basis: Limited SCADA estate and several process groups operating without confirmed supervisory visibility.

### 3.3 Data / Analytics [L-D3]

Score: 1-2. Label: Initial to Basic. Evidence basis: Manual time capture, unreliable Excel data, and absent database backbone constrain analytics.

### 3.4 Alarm Governance [L-D4]

Score: 1-2. Label: Initial to Basic. Evidence basis: Alarm-only protections and high alarm rates indicate weak governance and low automation enforcement.

### 3.5 Maintenance [L-D5]

Score: 2-3. Label: Basic to Structured. Evidence basis: PM and CBM procedures exist in scope, but spare-part delays and reactive conditions remain material.

### 3.6 Quality [L-D6]

Score: 2. Label: Basic. Evidence basis: Manual QC capture, staged approvals, and QA bottlenecks limit closed-loop quality governance.

### 3.7 Planning / Inventory [L-D7]

Score: 2. Label: Basic. Evidence basis: Planning relies on draft schedules, stock previews, and manual reconciliation across upstream/downstream plants.

### 3.8 Cybersecurity [L-D8]

Score: 1. Label: Initial / Ad-hoc. Evidence basis: Cybersecurity procedures are listed, but field evidence for segmentation and active controls is limited.

### 3.9 Vertical Integration [L-D9]

Score: 1-2. Label: Initial to Basic. Evidence basis: Manual file exchange and non-integrated equipment indicate weak OT-IT continuity.


## 4. Overall Maturity Profile

The weighted maturity baseline for Rodding Plant [INA-PL-RCP] is 1.9, which corresponds to Basic. This indicates that the plant has meaningful automation assets and documented governance constructs, but core execution, traceability, and supervisory integrity remain too fragmented for a higher classification.
